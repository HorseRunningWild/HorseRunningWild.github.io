Neural Structural Reasoner: A Brain-inspired Architecture for Reasoning over Structured Knowledge

Z. Jia, Y. Pan, N. Ji

Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2026.

A brain-inspired architecture that preserves relational structure in layered network connectivity, achieving competitive accuracy with human-readable reasoning traces over knowledge graphs.

Details & figures

NSR integrates hierarchical representation with relation-driven state transitions and Hebbian-type learning. Beyond benchmark accuracy, its inference dynamics natively expose human-readable reasoning traces, and the model spontaneously discovers interpretable latent structure — e.g., projecting nations by their learned relational connections yields four clusters that correspond to Cold-War political blocs.

Multimodal Fusion Network for Microdisplacement Measurement via Michelson Interferometer

Z. Jia, J. Li, X. Li et al.

Optics and Laser Technology 201 (2026) 115225 (CAS Zone 2 Top Optics Journal).

Developed a dual-head multimodal fusion network (MFN) to resolve lambda/2 phase ambiguity and achieve nanoscale displacement inference.

Details & figures

MFN fuses three interferogram channels (raw / differential / FFT) through a MobileViT-CNN hybrid backbone with an LSTM temporal encoder, then splits into a displacement regression head and an interference-order classification head. It maintains stable precision under severe noise while running over four orders of magnitude faster than the traditional HAA fitting pipeline.
